# Verse

Your music with lyrics that sync automatically.

## Get it

1. Download **[Verse-Setup.exe](../../releases/latest/download/Verse-Setup.exe)**.
2. Open it. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info** and then **Run anyway** (Verse isn't signed with a paid certificate).
3. Verse opens by itself. It finds the MP3s in your Downloads and Music folders, and you can add more folders from the library page.

Verse updates itself: when a new version is out, it downloads it in the background and asks you to restart.

## Spotify (optional)

Playing Spotify songs needs Spotify Premium and a Spotify developer app. Either ask to be added to an existing one, or make your own free one: Verse walks you through it when you click **Spotify → Connect** in the sidebar.

## Lyrics AI (optional)

On PCs with an NVIDIA graphics card, Settings → Lyrics → **Add the lyrics AI** downloads an AI that times lyrics to your files and writes lyrics for songs that have none online.
